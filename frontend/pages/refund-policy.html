Refund Policy

We want you to study with confidence. If our platform doesn't meet your expectations, we make the refund process simple and transparent.

Last updated: June 2026

Our commitment to you

You are eligible for a full refund within 14 days of purchase provided you have not accessed more than 20% of the exam content. We review every request fairly and aim to respond within 7 business days.

Eligibility

A full refund is available if all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your refund request is submitted within 14 calendar days of the original purchase date.
  • You have completed fewer than 20% of the questions in any practice exam included in your purchase.
  • You have not downloaded any study materials or exported exam results.

Partial refunds may be granted at our discretion for exceptional circumstances. If you are unsure whether you qualify, contact our support team before submitting a request.

How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, use our contact form below. Your name and email will be pre-filled if you are logged in. Include your order number (found in your purchase confirmation email from Paypal) and a brief description of your reason.

We will review your request and respond within 7 business days. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method and may take an additional 5-10 business days to appear depending on your bank.

Exceptions & Exclusions

The following purchases are not eligible for refunds:

  • Custom or negotiated corporate / enterprise packages.
  • Products purchased at a promotional or clearance discount of 50% or more.
  • Bundled products where one or more items have been substantially used.
  • Any purchase where exam attempt history has been accessed or study materials downloaded.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy, please visit our Help Center.
