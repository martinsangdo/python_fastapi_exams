Refund Policy

At ExamPrep we aim for student satisfaction. This page outlines when refunds are available.

Eligibility

Full refunds are available within 14 days of purchase if you have not accessed more than 20% of the course content. Partial refunds may be granted at our discretion.

How to Request a Refund

Contact support at support@example.com with your order number and reason for the request. We will review and respond within 5 business days.

Exceptions

Custom corporate packages, discounted bundled products, and promotional/clearance sales may be excluded from refunds. Accessing exam attempt history or downloading materials may void eligibility.
