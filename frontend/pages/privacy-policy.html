Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use CertQuestionBank.

Information We Collect

We collect account information (name, email), purchase and billing details, and anonymized usage data to improve our service. We do not sell your personal data.

How We Use Data

Data is used to provide course access, process payments, personalize recommendations, and measure learning outcomes. We may send transactional emails and occasional service updates.

Third Parties

We use third-party providers (payment processors, analytics) who process data on our behalf under contractual restrictions. Review their privacy practices for more details.

Your Choices

You can review or delete your account data by contacting support. Marketing emails can be opted out via links in the messages.
