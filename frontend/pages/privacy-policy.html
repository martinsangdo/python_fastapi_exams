Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have over your personal information.

Last updated: June 2026

We never sell your data

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or traded to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

You are in control

You can request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting our support team.

Data is protected

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

  • Account information — name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Purchase & billing details — transaction records and payment confirmation. Full card details are processed by our payment provider and never stored on our servers.
  • Learning activity — exam attempts, scores, time spent, and question-level responses used to power your progress dashboard.
  • Device & usage data — anonymised browser type, OS, and page interaction data used to improve platform performance.

How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Provide access to purchased exam content and track your learning progress.
  • Process payments and send purchase confirmation and receipts.
  • Personalise content recommendations based on your performance and study history.
  • Send transactional emails (account alerts, purchase confirmations) and, with your consent, product updates.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Measure and improve the quality and relevance of our content and platform.

Third-Party Services

We work with carefully selected third-party providers who process data on our behalf under binding contractual and confidentiality obligations. These include:

  • Payment processors — for secure transaction handling.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers — for hosting, storage, and content delivery.
  • Analytics services — for anonymised, aggregated usage analysis.
  • Email delivery services — for sending transactional and marketing communications.

We do not share personally identifiable information with any third party for independent marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain account and learning data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g. financial records).

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link in any message.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority in your jurisdiction.
